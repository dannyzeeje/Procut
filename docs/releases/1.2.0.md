## Phone editing layout

- The workspace picker sits with the project controls, playback fits on one strip, and Color and Audio keep their contextual tools in a fixed bottom dock.
- Compare outgoing and incoming frames in Precision Trim. Choose either edge, step the edit by one frame, or inspect the nearby footage in the filmstrip. Crop is available inside Transform.

## Color

- Quick Adjust follows the selected grading layer. Color Space Transform stays first in the layer stack, above the creative adjustments in processing order.
- Work on one color wheel or RGB channel at a time. Curves have larger handles and explicit point actions.
- Compare a paused frame with a draggable split. The before side keeps the camera input transform. Full-frame comparison remains available during playback.

## Audio

- Select a track or the master mix for a timed filter. The range follows the target in the timeline, and you can move it, drag its ends, or enter exact times.
- Set cutoff, audition in a loop, bypass, and apply in one undoable edit. Reopen the sound layer to change its filter, trim it, or remove it. Preview and export use the same track routing.
- The mixer shows side-by-side gain faders, mute, and solo, with exact gain and pan for the selected track.

The signed APK targets ARM64 devices with Android 8.0 or newer. Physical-device playback and touch checks are still pending.
