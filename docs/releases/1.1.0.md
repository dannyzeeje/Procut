## A roomier phone editor

- Switch between Edit, Color, and Audio from the top bar. The bottom toolbar adapts to the current selection, and batch mode keeps Copy, Split, Delete, and Done available throughout selection. Merge appears for compatible clips.
- Use the clip filmstrip and one-frame trim controls for precise edits. The right trim handle stops at the end of the available source, including on retimed clips. Common transform settings have larger controls and exact entry.

## Color and LUTs

- Loop the selected shot while grading and compare before and after in the same preview. Grade with ordered clip layers and use separate RGB gamma and gain controls, wheels, and curves.
- Color Space Transform adds Apple Log and ARRI LogC3 EI 800 alongside the Sony S-Log3 and Panasonic V-Log presets.
- Import individual creative LUTs or folders of .cube files. Collections become tabs, and LUTs can be moved between collections or removed from the picker. Projects that already use a removed LUT can still render it.

## Audio and playback

- Select an audio range with draggable markers, exact time entry, or one-frame steps. Loop the selection to audition it and apply a low-pass or high-pass filter to the master mix for that span.
- Remove an animation loop from the Loops panel. Playback keeps advancing while audio output is unavailable, and Optical Flow progress stays visible while the speed panel is open.

The APK targets ARM64 Android devices running Android 8.0 or later. Host tests passed for source-limited trims, merge undo, playback clock recovery, Reverse, and Optical Flow. Playback, export, and narrow-screen interaction on a physical Android device are still pending.
